Home answers what is happening today. Insights answers the questions an owner asks at closing time: how the week went, whether the month's costs are covered, who to call, and what is sitting unsold. It lives under More, and only partners can open it. Staff never see it. It writes nothing of its own: every answer is read from the sales, debts, stock and costs the app already records.

### Who to call first

A debt's weight is its size times its age, the days since the customer's oldest unpaid sale. So a small debt that has waited for months can rank above a larger one from last week. Each customer also shows how long they have owed and, where a due date has passed, how many days past due. The colour follows how long they have owed: fresh up to a week, needing attention up to two months, and at risk after that.

### When the month's costs are covered

A month's fixed costs are its rent, its wages and its utilities. When the rent is set up in the app, its monthly rate counts, not what was typed by hand. The screen names the first day on which the month's running profit reached those costs.

### The month and the year

By month opens any month back to the first sale: sales, cost of goods and profit, the month by category, where the money went against the month before, the best and weakest products, and the discounts. A month still running is compared with the same days of the month before, so half a month is never set against a whole one. The page says when its figures were read, and says plainly when they were read offline and may be incomplete.

The Year summary gives one row per month with sales, cost of goods, expenses and net profit, and the year's totals. For the current year it adds the credit still owed to the shop today. It can be shared as an image, for the accountant. On the Orders tab, partners get sales and profit by month as well, one line per sale with what it sold for, what the goods cost and what it earned.

<ForEngineers>
  Insights runs six reads in parallel: recent orders, products, debtors, open credit orders, fixed-cost expense rows and rent. They succeed or fail together. One pure function then folds them into every section, so each rule is a unit test away. Insights writes no document of its own. The month and year pages, like the drawer and the day ledgers, read the time from one injected clock, and a guard fails the suite if any of their files calls the wall clock, because a span of "the same days of the month before" can only be tested with a fixed clock. The year image is the table painted at three times its size and shared as a PNG with no new package, and sharing is off when the figures were read offline.
</ForEngineers>
